Buying off-plan, explained simply

Off-plan means buying a home before it's built, or while it's under construction, usually directly from the developer. Instead of paying in full upfront, you pay in stages.

Why it appeals, and what to weigh Staged payments can spread the cost, but completion dates can move, and you're committing to something you can't yet see. Always read the sales agreement with independent legal advice.
  1. 1

    Choose and reserve

    You pick a unit in a registered project and usually pay a reservation deposit. A licensed broker can explain the options and paperwork.

  2. 2

    Sign and register

    You sign a Sales and Purchase Agreement (SPA). Off-plan sales in Dubai are recorded with the Dubai Land Department's interim register, known as Oqood, and registration fees apply.

  3. 3

    Pay in stages

    Payments follow the agreed schedule, often tied to construction milestones. Dubai law requires off-plan buyer payments to go into a regulated project escrow account.

  4. 4

    Handover

    On completion you inspect the property, settle any remaining balance and receive the title deed. Some plans continue payments after handover.

Questions UK buyers ask

General information only. For your situation, speak to a licensed broker, a UAE property lawyer and a UK tax adviser.

Can UK citizens buy property in Dubai?

Generally, yes. Foreign nationals can buy freehold property in areas of Dubai designated for foreign ownership. A licensed broker and conveyancer can confirm the rules for a specific property.

Are you a real estate broker?

No. Dubai Property Lens is an educational platform. We don't sell, list or market specific properties, and we don't handle money. With your consent, we introduce interested buyers to appropriately licensed Dubai real estate brokerage partners.

How are off-plan payments protected?

Dubai requires developers to register off-plan projects and to hold buyer payments in a regulated escrow account for the project. Check that a project is registered and that you pay into the correct escrow account. An independent lawyer can review this for you.

Do I need to travel to Dubai to buy?

Not always. Many steps can be completed remotely, sometimes using a power of attorney. Requirements depend on the developer and the transaction, so confirm with your broker and lawyer.

Do I pay UK tax on Dubai rental income or gains?

UK residents are generally taxed on worldwide income and gains, so overseas rental income or a profit on sale may need to be reported to HMRC. Rules depend on your residence and circumstances. Speak to a qualified UK tax adviser.

Can I get a mortgage as a UK resident?

Some UAE lenders offer mortgages to non-residents, subject to their own criteria. Off-plan purchases are often funded through the developer's payment plan instead. A regulated mortgage adviser can explain the options.

Does buying property give me a UAE visa?

The UAE offers some residence visas linked to property ownership. Eligibility criteria are set by the authorities and can change, so check the official government sources or ask a licensed adviser.
