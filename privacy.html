What we collect

  • your phone or WhatsApp number, if you choose to give it
  • where you are based, your budget range, your main goal and when you are thinking of buying
  • your choices on the two optional consent boxes

Information collected automatically with your form:

  • the choices you made on our page before opening the form, such as your budget, goal, property type, or a project you asked about
  • campaign details from the link you clicked to reach us, such as UTM tags and ad click identifiers (gclid, fbclid)

The investment plan calculator on our website runs in your browser. The figures you type are kept only on your own device and are not sent to us.

We do not ask for, and you should not send us, sensitive information such as health details, or financial account numbers.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • ask us to correct data that is wrong or incomplete
  • ask us to delete your data
  • ask us to restrict how we use your data
  • object to our use of your data based on legitimate interests, and to direct marketing at any time
  • receive data you gave us in a portable format
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting what we did before you withdrew it

To use any of these rights, email [contact email]. We will reply within one month. There is usually no charge.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, please tell us first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

Cookies and similar technologies

Our website does not set advertising or analytics cookies.

  • Your device's storage: the calculator and property finder save your entries in your browser's local storage, so they are still there when you come back. This stays on your device; you can clear it in your browser settings.
  • Embedded form: our form provider, Tally, may use cookies or similar technologies that are needed for the form to work.
  • Fonts: our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers and shares your IP address with Google.

If we add analytics or advertising tools in future, we will ask for your consent first through a cookie banner and update this notice.
